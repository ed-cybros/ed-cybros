## Applied Data Projects

### Data Extraction & Ingestion

**[TES Alchemy Parser 1.0](https://github.com/ed-cybros/tes-alchemy-parser-v1.0)**

* Built automated scraping pipelines using Requests and BeautifulSoup.
* Extracted reagent and effect information from Elder Scrolls series online wiki.
* Consolidated alchemy information from multiple Elder Scrolls titles into a common dataset.

**[TES Alchemy Parser 2.0](https://github.com/ed-cybros/tes-alchemy-parser-v2.0)**

* Expanded the original scraping pipeline to collect additional information.
* Introduced structured intermediate storage (dictionary) to support more reliable transformation processes.

### Data Normalization & Quality

**TES Alchemy Parser 1.0**

* Identified equivalent alchemy effects represented under different names across games.
* Designed a unified naming scheme and applied it to standardize effect names.
* Removed duplicate and inconsistent records while preserving reagent-effect associations.

**TES Alchemy Parser 2.0**

* Refactored transformation procedures for better efficiency and data preservation.
* Introduced validation checks to review transformation results and verify dataset integrity.
* Improved data quality and dataset consistency.

### Data Structuring & Storage

**TES Alchemy Parser 1.0**

* Used TXT files with formatting and marking as intermediate layers.
* Produced a cleaned and standardized formatted text-based dataset for manual lookup.

**TES Alchemy Parser 2.0**

* Introduced JSON as an intermediate transformation layer.
* Designed a normalized SQLite database with many-to-many relationship to store transformed data.
* Provided flexible querying and data exploration.
* Performed integrity checks to verify database consistency.

## Direction

My medium-term goal is to develop into a **data-centric machine learning** role at the intersection of data quality, feature design, and domain understanding.

Long-term, to apply the acquired principles to **music data**. I aim to unite machine learning, signal processing, and human perception to derive meaningful information from complex musical signals.

## Interest in domain

I aim to work with **music data** and the music industry.

My areas of interest are:

- Music-related datasets and structure
- Music information retrieval (MIR)
- Digital signal processing (DSP)
- Musicology
- Recommendation and discovery systems

and anything to help analyze, explore, and understand music more deeply.

I am interested in how content-aware machine learning systems can improve **music discovery** and help address long-tail challenges in large music catalogs, with a special focus on learning to **identify underrepresented but high-quality content**.

## Working style
